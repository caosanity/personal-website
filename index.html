About Me

I'm a Production Manager with 5 years of experience leading cross-functional teams through complex operational challenges — from high-volume manufacturing to fast-paced media production. I thrive on building systems that scale, removing friction from workflows, and getting the most out of every team I work with.

Over the past few years, I've become increasingly fascinated by artificial intelligence — not as a buzzword, but as a practical lens for solving problems I encounter every day. I explore how AI tools can accelerate decision-making, surface insights from messy data, and handle repetitive work so that humans can focus on what actually matters.

I believe the best Production Managers of the next decade will be the ones who understand how to work with intelligent systems, not just alongside them. That's what drives my curiosity and shapes my work.
