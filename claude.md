# CLAUDE.md — Personal Website

## Project Overview

This is a personal website for a **Production Manager with a passion for AI**. The site serves as a professional hub to showcase identity, work, and thinking — blending operational expertise with forward-looking interest in artificial intelligence.

---

## Site Identity

- **Owner:** [Your Name]
- **Role:** Production Manager
- **Tagline:** *Bridging operations and intelligence.*
- **Tone:** Professional yet approachable. Clear and confident. Curious and forward-thinking.
- **Audience:** Recruiters, collaborators, fellow PMs, AI enthusiasts, and potential clients.

---

## Site Structure

The website has three main sections:

### 1. About Me
- A short personal bio introducing who you are as a Production Manager
- Highlight your background: industries worked in, core skills (operations, workflow optimization, cross-functional leadership), and years of experience
- Describe your passion for AI: what draws you to it, how you apply it or explore it in your work
- Include a professional photo placeholder
- Optional: a "Currently" section (what you're working on / learning right now)

### 2. Projects
- Showcase 3–6 key projects, each with:
  - **Title** and **short description**
  - **Your role** (e.g., Production Manager, Lead, Contributor)
  - **Key outcomes** (metrics, impact, or learnings)
  - **Tags** (e.g., `AI`, `Operations`, `Automation`, `Workflow Design`)
  - Optional: link to case study, GitHub repo, or external resource
- Mix of production management projects and AI-adjacent experiments or tools you've explored/built

### 3. Posts
- A collection of written content — articles, reflections, or short-form notes
- Each post should have:
  - **Title**
  - **Date**
  - **Short summary / excerpt**
  - **Tags** (e.g., `AI`, `Product`, `Industry Thoughts`)
  - Link to full post
- Topics may include: AI trends in production, lessons from managing complex projects, tools and workflows, reflections on human + AI collaboration

---

## Design Direction

- **Style:** Clean, minimal, modern — with subtle AI/tech-forward aesthetic
- **Color palette:** Neutral base (white or off-white) with one strong accent color (e.g., electric blue, teal, or warm amber)
- **Typography:** Sans-serif for body (e.g., Inter, DM Sans); slightly bolder for headers
- **Layout:** Single-page scroll with anchor nav, or multi-page with persistent header
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first design

---

## Tech Stack (Suggested)

- **Framework:** Next.js or Astro (great for static personal sites with blog support)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Content:** Markdown files for Posts, JSON or MDX for Projects
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Netlify
- **Analytics:** Plausible or Fathom (privacy-friendly)

---

## Key Pages / Routes

| Route | Description |
|---|---|
| `/` | Hero + About Me section |
| `/projects` | Grid or list of projects |
| `/posts` | Blog/post index |
| `/posts/[slug]` | Individual post page |

---

## Content Principles

- Write in **first person** — this is a personal site, not a corporate one
- Keep copy **concise** — lead with impact, explain context second
- Use **AI as a lens**, not a gimmick — show thoughtful engagement, not buzzword-dropping
- Update the Projects and Posts sections **regularly** to keep the site alive
